# Quiet Hours: same visual identity, a new place and sound each day

The user's Night Rain thumbnail is the channel's **design reference**, not a
scene to recreate. A warm lamp, porch, wet garden and thunder must not become
the backdrop for every episode. Recoloring, recropping or retitling that picture
does not create a new daily idea.

Keep the photographic finish, very large condensed cream title, and rounded
gold **1 HOUR** badge with black lettering. Choose the title placement to fit
each new composition. Use natural habitat-appropriate colors: forest dawn can
be green and gold; open water can be silver and blue. Every thumbnail should
remain recognizable at phone size.

Change the soundscape, physical setting, viewpoint and focal subject. A quiet
pebble cove, misty forest, reed-lined lake, summer meadow and mossy stream should
look and sound distinct. Audio comes from documented real stereo field
recordings with verified commercial-use permission. Do not synthesize a new
noise bed and label it a nature recording. Do not invent recording locations or
licences. AI-created scenery is disclosed separately from real recorded audio.

Every full-hour MP4 and both promotional Shorts must now show **clearly visible
movement within the scene**, with a fixed camera. For shore scenes, water must
wash onto the shore and recede; for rain scenes, rain must visibly fall, with
appropriate splashes and ripples. Streams need directional water flow, while
wildlife settings can use natural movement in foliage, reeds or water without
inventing visible animals. Each concept has its own `motion_requirements` brief.

A still image held for the export, a pan or zoom across a still, moving text, or
changing brightness alone does not meet this requirement. There is no static
fallback. Still backdrops may remain source artwork or downloadable images, and
thumbnails remain images; the MP4 exports must contain the visible scene motion.
Review the actual full export and both Shorts at normal viewing size, checking
that each vertical crop retains the moving subject and the motion fits the
soundtrack. This is a requirement for future production, not a claim that an
existing file has passed that review.

`config/brand-style.json` is the reusable specification.
`config/daily-concepts.json` is a fourteen-episode idea queue beginning
4 November 2026 in Asia/Nicosia. These are concepts awaiting source/asset
verification, not fourteen finished videos. The planner stops at the end of the
queue; it must not cycle silently back to an old image or soundscape.

Every concept also needs three distinct publishing entries: a title and
description for the full hour, and a title and description for each Short.
`config/daily-publishing-copy.json` supplies this copy to the daily planner.
Use sound-specific language, a calm tone and accurate scene disclosure. Avoid
promises about sleep or health, invented recording locations, unsupported
species names, and claims such as "no ads." Append the actual recording credits
and licence/change notices for the audio used in each export. A drafted
description is not evidence that its recordings or permissions already exist.

The first new concept is **Quiet Shore**: close lapping water at a sheltered
pebble cove, a small amber-window beach hut viewed from outside, and blue-hour
light. There is no porch, rain or lightning. The text-free backdrop and branded
thumbnail are separate assets.

Daily Filebin delivery, including each concept's publishing copy, is authorized
with a confirmed **18:00 Asia/Nicosia** target. Automation remains inactive
until a working cloud runner is deployed and the required assets are prepared.
Available image-generation tools can prepare artwork
in this chat; the command-line daily job cannot silently call a paid image API
or generate future licensed recordings. A cloud run uses the verified assets
already prepared for that dated episode. YouTube uploads currently remain
manual because no channel authorization is connected here.
