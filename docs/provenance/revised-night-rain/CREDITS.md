## Processing and source quality

The source media are the complete high-quality stereo MP3 previews publicly served by Freesound. These are compressed distribution copies, not the creators’ original WAV files. The acquired bytes, hashes, durations and channel counts are in `sources.json`. Four different passages are used once each, joined with 16-second equal-power crossfades. The opening features RossJuterbock’s close roof drips and puddles, followed by joedeshon’s porch rain, lastraindrop’s umbrella recording, and TRP’s storm. The mix retains original stereo information, uses constant gain to match passage loudness, and applies measured constant gain plus an oversampled lookahead limiter for strong peaks. A final constant 0.5 dB gain uses remaining headroom. The verified master measures −22.9 LUFS integrated, −4.0 dBTP true peak and 13.2 LU loudness range, retaining natural changes in the rainfall. There is no synthesized rain and no repeated five-minute prototype. Natural thunder remains part of the original continuous takes; no thunder sample is repeated or newly inserted. The finished mix sidecar records exact source/timeline intervals and measured loudness.

Source 244359 by BOBtheROSS was downloaded and permission-checked as a candidate but is not used in this mix. Mono source 623847 by Straal99 was rejected for the stereo requirement. The attached five-minute prototype is not used.

## Review limits

Creator descriptions establish field-recording provenance; FFprobe, hashes, signal analysis, full decoding and loudness checks establish technical properties. These checks do not substitute for human listening to judge the sound, background noise, naturalness or subjective transition quality. Audit excerpts are supplied in `outputs/revised-night-rain/audio-audit/`.
