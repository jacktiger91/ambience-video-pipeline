Changes: passages selected from the original recordings, level matched, joined with 16-second crossfades, adjusted to a comfortable measured loudness with true-peak headroom, and encoded as a stereo soundtrack. The bird and woodland sounds were recorded in the actual locations credited above. A separate backdrop must not be represented as footage of those recording locations unless its provenance establishes that.

Only credit sources actually present in each Short. Source selection and the final edit decision list determine the relevant recordist and link.

Quality review limitation: event classification and loudness measurements are objective technical checks, not a claim of subjective listening.
