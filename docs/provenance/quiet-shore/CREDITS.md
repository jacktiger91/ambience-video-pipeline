## Changes and distribution quality

Four distinct source intervals are arranged once each into one hour. The two Whitby intervals do not overlap. Intervals containing detected voices, a transient impact or a possible late boat are excluded; exact edit decisions are in `edit-decision-list.json`. The transitions use16-second equal-power crossfades. Source passages are level-matched by constant gain, blended through floating-point PCM to preserve headroom, then adjusted with measured constant gain and a lookahead peak limiter. No rain, thunder, music or birds are added. No recording from Night Rain is reused.

Whitby and Dunwich use the collection’s MP3 files. De Haan uses the archive’s listed Ogg Vorbis derivative because its original MP3 download returned a server error. These are compressed distribution sources, not original uncompressed microphone masters; the finished FLAC preserves the resulting mix without additional lossy encoding.

## Checks and limitations

Creator descriptions establish field-recording provenance. Full-source and selected-interval sound-event classification, channel analysis, loudness measurements and end-to-end decoding provide automated quality checks. They do not substitute for subjective human listening. The full video and two Shorts are intended for final review before publication.

The downloaded Blackpool recording is a backup candidate and is not used. The inaccessible Freesound candidates and the earlier rain recordings are not used.
